Runs with: path_data_model(command: "get-business-object", args: "<flags>") (read-only).

Usage: get-business-object [options]

Show one business object: names, type, identifier field, menu/messages/email
switches, and its context (the designer's description of what it is for)

Options:
  --bo-id <id>      Business object ID
  --bo-name <name>  Business object name
